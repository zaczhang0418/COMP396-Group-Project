## Core Argument To Preserve

- V2 was weak on Part 3 not because of one isolated bad trade, but because several portfolio-level weaknesses aligned.
- The equity and drawdown evidence showed low resilience under stress.
- The structural evidence showed static asset binding and weak realised diversification.
- The execution evidence showed persistent capital pressure and repeated rebalancing churn.
- The summary metrics confirmed that these weaknesses translated into inferior return-risk and execution quality relative to the later V3 refinement.
